Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy describes how Galactic Conquest (“the game”, package com.netmedia.galactic_conquest), operated by Netmedia808 (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use our Android app and related online services.

Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and authentication identifiers from Firebase Authentication (including Google Sign-In when you choose that option).
  • Game profile: callsign, combat record, defenses, recon reports, battle outcomes, rankings, Titanium balance, and similar gameplay state stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Device data: push notification tokens if you allow notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Technical data: basic service logs from Firebase / Cloud Functions needed to operate multiplayer features, prevent abuse, and fix errors.

How we use information

  • Provide gameplay, matchmaking, combat resolution, and leaderboards.
  • Maintain your commander profile and sync progress across sessions.
  • Send optional push notifications you enable.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and protect the integrity of the game.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where applicable, we process data to perform our contract with you (providing the game), for legitimate interests (security, anti-cheat, analytics at service level), and with consent where required (e.g. optional notifications).

Third-party processors

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) and Google Play for distribution. Their policies also apply: Firebase Privacy, Google Privacy Policy.

Retention

We keep gameplay and profile data while your account is active. After a verified deletion request, we delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where law or fraud prevention requires limited retention.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Contact us to request removal if you believe we have.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from Commander Record → Settings.
  • Disable push notifications in device or in-app settings.
  • Request account and data deletion — see Delete account.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@netmedia808.com
