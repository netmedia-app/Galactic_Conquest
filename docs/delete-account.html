Delete account & data

This page satisfies Google Play’s data deletion requirement and explains how to remove your Galactic Conquest commander profile.

What is deleted

After we verify your request, we delete or deactivate:

  • Firebase Authentication record for your sign-in (email / Google-linked account).
  • Commander profile: callsign, defenses, recon history, rankings snapshot, Titanium, and related Firestore data.
  • Push notification device registrations linked to your account.

Anonymized battle statistics may remain in aggregate logs where they no longer identify you. Backups may retain data for a limited period before automatic purge.

How to request deletion

  1. Sign in to the game with the account you want removed (so we can verify ownership).
  2. Email support@netmedia808.com from the same email used to register (or state your Google account email).
  3. Include subject line “Galactic Conquest account deletion” and your callsign if you have one.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and will confirm by email.

Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent — you cannot recover the same callsign or history.
  • Sign out on all devices after confirmation if you still have the app installed.
  • If you only want to stop playing, you may sign out without deleting data (Commander Record → Settings).
